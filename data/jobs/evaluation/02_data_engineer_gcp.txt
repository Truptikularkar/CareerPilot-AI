Job Title: Data Engineer — GCP & BigQuery
Company: Enterprise DataWave Solutions
Location: Pune, India / Remote
Experience Required: 2-3 years

Job Summary:
Enterprise DataWave is seeking a Data Engineer to optimize analytics pipelines and manage enterprise data warehouses on Google Cloud.

Key Responsibilities:
- Build batch and real-time ETL pipelines ingesting hundreds of thousands of events daily into BigQuery.
- Optimize BigQuery query performance and cost via table partitioning and clustering.
- Automate data pipeline scheduling using Apache Airflow DAGs.
- Implement automated data quality checks and duplicate detection logic in SQL.

Requirements (Must Have):
- 2+ years of data engineering experience with Python and SQL.
- Strong proficiency in Google BigQuery, table design, and performance optimization.
- Experience with Apache Airflow workflow orchestration.
- Experience with GCP services including Cloud Storage and Pub/Sub.

Preferred (Nice to Have):
- Experience integrating AI-based alerting or automated remediation.
- Familiarity with CI/CD deployment pipelines.
