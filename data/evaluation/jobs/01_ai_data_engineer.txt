Job Title: AI Data Engineer
Company: CognitiveScale Labs
Location: Pune, Maharashtra (Hybrid)
Employment Type: Full-Time
Experience Required: 2-4 years

About the Role:
CognitiveScale Labs is expanding our data and AI platform team. We are looking for an AI Data Engineer to build high-throughput data validation pipelines and integrate LLM-based agentic workflows into our core data ecosystem.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and optimize automated ETL/ELT pipelines using Python, SQL, and Google BigQuery.
- Author and maintain robust workflow orchestration DAGs in Apache Airflow.
- Build and maintain data validation frameworks enforcing schema consistency, row-count reconciliation, and null checks.
- Integrate generative AI models (Vertex AI, Gemini API) and RAG workflows into production data monitoring pipelines.
- Implement event-driven ingestion using Cloud Storage, Pub/Sub, and Cloud Functions.

Required Qualifications (Must Have):
- 2+ years of production experience in Python and advanced SQL.
- Hands-on experience with Google Cloud Platform (GCP) including BigQuery and Cloud Storage.
- Proven experience authoring DAGs in Apache Airflow.
- Hands-on experience or demonstrated project work with LLMs, RAG, and AI agents.
- Strong focus on data quality, data validation, and automated pipeline monitoring.

Preferred Qualifications (Nice to Have):
- Experience with BigQuery ML for anomaly detection.
- Familiarity with vector stores (FAISS, ChromaDB) and hybrid retrieval techniques.
- Knowledge of containerization with Docker.
